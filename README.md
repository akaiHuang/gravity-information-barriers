## Abstract

We show that the gravitational entropy production Sigma = -ln(-g_00), derived from channel extensivity, satisfies the vacuum equation nabla^2 Sigma = 0. The maximum principle for this harmonic field establishes three barriers to exotic spacetime structures, each with a quantifiable "price" to overcome:

1. **Event horizons** require stellar-mass energy concentration -- a price Nature already pays
2. **Warp bubbles** require E ~ v^4 R^2 / delta of localized energy -- enormously expensive but finite
3. **Closed timelike curves** require Sigma < 0 (time running faster than at infinity) -- the most expensive barrier, requiring physics beyond the harmonic approximation

We compute each price explicitly and identify experimental signatures at the boundary of Barrier 1 testable by the Event Horizon Telescope.

## Key Results

- **Sigma = -ln(-g_00)** is the unique extensive entropy production of the gravitational channel (Cauchy functional equation)
- **nabla^2 Sigma = 0** (Fisher information minimization) gives the exponential metric g_00 = -exp(-r_s/r) with NO event horizon
- **Maximum principle** simultaneously forbids horizons, warp bubbles, and CTCs in the harmonic approximation
- **Shadow prediction**: b_c = e * r_s = 2.72 r_s (+4.6% vs Schwarzschild 2.60 r_s) -- testable by ngEHT
- **Warp energy**: E ~ v^4 (subluminal warp is exponentially cheaper; walking speed = 60 MJ)

## Important Caveat

nabla^2 Sigma = 0 is an **alternative** vacuum principle, not a consequence of Einstein's equations. Einstein's equations give Schwarzschild (with horizon); Fisher information minimization gives the exponential metric (without horizon). Both agree through 2PN order. Which is correct is an empirical question.
